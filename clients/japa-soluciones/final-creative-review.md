# Final Creative Review — JAPA Soluciones

## Decision
**Approve for Client review. WordPress-ready technically. Block Publish-ready.**

## What works
- The page is clear, direct and oriented to a useful quote rather than vague sales language.
- Technical visual cues feel appropriate without simulating certifications or projects.
- The architecture explains services, information required and next step efficiently.
- Placeholders and inactive WhatsApp state prevent accidental misrepresentation.

## What feels generic
- Without real service scope, projects, coverage and proof, JAPA can still feel interchangeable with other contractors.
- “Clarity” and “trust” become meaningful only when the client confirms a real process and evidence.

## Strengthen next
1. Confirm the specific profitable services and exclusions.
2. Add project photography and proof with permission.
3. Define coverage, response process and why JAPA is the right choice.
4. Activate a tested WhatsApp quote flow.

## Conversion risks
No operational CTA, price/qualification guidance, coverage or response expectation. Leads cannot yet know whether JAPA is a fit.

## Visual risks
Real photos may vary in quality and break the current rhythm; define crops and image standards before replacing placeholders.

## Blockers
WhatsApp, service catalog, coverage, images/permissions, trust proof, commercial process, policies/legal, SEO local data and production QA.

## Final recommendation
Present the preview to confirm structure, tone and service priorities. Do not add more visual complexity; use real evidence to make the next iteration distinctive and publishable.
